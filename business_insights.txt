Insights:
1. Technology is the top-performing category, driven heavily by laptop sales.
2. Office Supplies has the highest order volume but the lowest revenue per order, due to low unit prices.
3. The South region leads in total sales, more than double the North region.
4. Sales and Profit are strongly correlated (0.81) - high-value orders reliably generate proportional profit.
5. May was the peak sales month, coinciding with a large bulk laptop order (O1022, $2,160).

Highest/Lowest:
Highest category - Technology (7,110 in sales)
Lowest category - Office Supplies (539 in sales)
Highest region - South
Lowest region - North

Recommendations:
1. Expand Technology inventory and marketing, since it drives the most revenue and profit.
2. Investigate underperformance in the North region - targeted promotions could help close the gap with South.
